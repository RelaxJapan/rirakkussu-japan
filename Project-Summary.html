This document summarises the work completed on the Rirakkusu Japan website — a fully designed, responsive marketing site with a custom multi-step booking system that captures client enquiries for both service divisions (Event Planning & Management and Personal & Corporate Project Management).

  • Team section with the correct member photos and the "Joining Soon" roles.

Sections included

Responsive

  • Fully optimised for mobile phones, tablets and desktop — tested with no layout or overflow issues.
  • Mobile navigation menu, stacked layouts, and scaled typography for small screens.

2. Online Booking System

A custom, step-by-step booking tool was built so clients can request a service online and the team receives every detail — directly reflecting the services document provided.

How it works for the customer

StepWhat the customer does
1 · ServiceChooses Event Planning & Management or Personal & Corporate Project Management
2 · TypePicks the specific service (Picnic, Anniversary, Date, Dinner, Ceremony, Celebration — or Personal Task, Personal Assistant, Virtual Assistant)
3 · DetailsSelects their preferences from dropdown menus — venue, guests, portion, cuisine (Chūshin), drinks, other services, booking vs. consultation, etc.
4 · Their infoProvides name, email, phone, date of birth, nationality and residential address
5 · ReviewConfirms a summary and submits

What the business receives

  • Every submission delivers the chosen service, all selected options, and the customer's full contact details straight to the company inbox and a secure online dashboard.
  • This gives the team exactly what they need to follow up, confirm bookings, and send gifts — knowing precisely who requested what.
  • The service options, cuisines, venues and categories all come directly from the brand's services document.

3. Contact Form
